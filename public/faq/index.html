Services

What services does Mogeny provide?

What does Mogeny LLC do?

Mogeny LLC is a financial management firm in Chicago, IL. We provide financial management and reporting, bookkeeping, controller and CFO services, family office and estate management, KPI analysis and forensic accounting for businesses and families.

Do you prepare tax returns?

Tax preparation is not one of the services Mogeny LLC offers. We keep your books and reports accurate and organized so your tax preparer has what they need at year end.

What is the difference between a controller and a CFO?

A controller makes sure the numbers are right, by running the month-end close and reviewing the books. A CFO uses those numbers to plan cash, budgets and growth.

Do you give investment or legal advice?

No. We work alongside your investment adviser and your attorney and give them accurate numbers.

What is forensic accounting?

Forensic accounting is the close examination of financial records to find out what happened, often when money is missing or disputed.

Pricing

How does pricing work?

How much do your services cost?

The price depends on the work, how often it happens and how many accounts and entities are involved. We give you the scope and price in writing before any work starts. Call (773) 964-2446 to begin.

Is catch-up bookkeeping priced separately?

Yes. Catch-up work is a one-off project, priced on its own, separate from the monthly bookkeeping that follows.

Remote clients

Can Mogeny work with me remotely?

Do you only work with clients in Chicago?

No. We work in person with clients in Chicago and remotely with clients anywhere in the US.

How does remote work with Mogeny work?

We meet by video call or phone, exchange documents by email or a shared folder, and work in your accounting software with the access you give us.

Which time zone do you work in?

Mogeny is in Chicago, on Central Time. We schedule calls at times that work for both of us.

Privacy

How is my information handled?

What should I not send through the website?

Please never send passwords, account numbers or Social Security numbers through the form or by regular email. We will arrange a safer way to share anything sensitive.

Who sees my financial information?

Only the people at Mogeny who do your work, and the people you name, such as your tax preparer or attorney.
