Getting started

What happens after you get in touch?

Starting with Mogeny takes 4 steps, and you decide at each one whether to go ahead.

  1. You call or send the form

Read all questions and answers

What does Mogeny LLC do?

Mogeny LLC is a financial management firm in Chicago, IL. We provide financial management and reporting, bookkeeping, controller and CFO services, family office and estate management, KPI analysis and forensic accounting for businesses and families.

Do you only work with businesses in Chicago?

No. We work in person with clients in Chicago and remotely with clients anywhere in the US.

How much do your services cost?

The price depends on the work, how often it happens and how many accounts and entities are involved. We talk through what you need first, then give you the scope and price before any work starts. Call (773) 964-2446 to begin.

Can you catch up books that are months or years behind?

Yes. Catch-up bookkeeping rebuilds the missing records from your bank and card statements and reconciles each month. Once the books are current, we can keep them that way.

Do you prepare tax returns?

Tax preparation is not one of the services Mogeny LLC offers. We keep your books and reports accurate and organized so your tax preparer has what they need at year end.

What do I need for the first conversation?

Nothing formal. A short description of your business or family finances and what is not working is enough. If you have recent statements or reports, they help, but you do not need them to call.
