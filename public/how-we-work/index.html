How we work

What to expect when you start with Mogeny

Starting with Mogeny LLC, a Chicago, IL financial management firm, takes 4 steps. You get in touch, we talk through your finances on an introductory call, you receive a written proposal with the scope and price, and then we set up access and begin. You decide at each step whether to go ahead.

Step by step

What happens at each step?

From the first call to the first monthly report.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Call or text (773) 964-2446, email hello@mogeny.com, or send the form. A few sentences about your business or family and what is not working is enough. We reply to set up an introductory call.

  2. 02

    Introductory call

    We ask about your accounts, your software, the reports you have now and the deadlines you face. You ask us anything you want to know about how we work. Chicago clients can meet in person if they prefer.

  3. 03

    Written proposal

    You receive a proposal that sets out the work, how often it happens and the price. Nothing starts until you agree to it.

  4. 04

    Setup and first month

    We arrange access to the accounts and software you agree to share, catch up anything that is behind, and start the regular work. Your first reports follow once the first month is closed.

Before the call

What should you have ready for the first call?

None of this is required. If you have none of it, that is a good reason to call.

  • A short description of your business or family finances
  • The accounting software you use, if any
  • How many bank and credit card accounts you have
  • Any deadlines, such as a loan application, a tax filing or a sale
  • Recent financial reports or statements, if you have them
Staying in touch

How do we keep in touch once work starts?

You get regular check-ins on a schedule agreed with you, by phone, video call or in person in Chicago. Between check-ins, you can call or email with questions.

Security

How is your information kept safe?

We ask only for the access the work needs and use read-only access wherever a bank or software offers it. Please never send passwords, account numbers or Social Security numbers through this website or by regular email.

Questions

What do people ask about getting started?

How long does it take to get started?

It depends on how much catch-up work is needed. Setup can start as soon as you accept the proposal and share access.

Can I meet in person?

Yes, if you are in Chicago. Clients elsewhere in the US work with us by video call, phone and email.

What if I am not sure which service I need?

That is what the introductory call is for. Describe what is going on and we will suggest where to start.

Do I have to have my records organized before I call?

No. Many people call because their records are not organized. Sorting them out is part of the work.
