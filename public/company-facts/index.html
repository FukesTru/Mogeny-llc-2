Each service

What does each Mogeny service do?

  • Financial management and reporting. Mogeny LLC prepares monthly financial statements, budgets and cash flow reports and explains them in plain words.
  • Bookkeeping. Mogeny LLC records, categorizes and reconciles transactions each month and brings books that are behind up to date.
  • Controller and CFO services. Mogeny LLC provides part-time controller and CFO support, including month-end close, cash forecasts and budgets.
  • Family office and estate management. Mogeny LLC tracks and reports household, property, trust and estate finances alongside the family’s attorney and advisers.
  • Data-driven KPI analysis. Mogeny LLC chooses and tracks the key performance indicators that show how a business is doing against its goals.
  • Forensic accounting. Mogeny LLC examines financial records when money is missing, disputed or unexplained and reports the findings in writing.
