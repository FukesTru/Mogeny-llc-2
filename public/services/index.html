How it fits together

How do the services fit together?

Each service builds on the one before it. You can start at any level, but the numbers always rest on clean books.

  1. Bookkeeping

    Records and reconciles every transaction, so the books are complete and correct.

  2. Financial reporting

    Turns the closed books into monthly statements and explains what changed.

  3. KPI analysis

    Picks the few numbers that matter most for your goals and tracks them closely.

  4. Controller and CFO

    Checks that the numbers can be trusted, then uses them to plan cash, budgets and growth.

Questions

What do people ask about our services?

Can I use more than one service?

Yes. You can start with one service, such as bookkeeping, and add reporting, KPI analysis or CFO support later.

Do you work with both businesses and families?

Yes. Mogeny works with business owners on their companies and with families on household, property and estate finances.

Are all services available remotely?

Yes. Every service is available to clients anywhere in the US. Chicago clients can also meet in person.

Which service should I start with?

If your books are behind or unreliable, start with bookkeeping. If the books are in good shape, start with reporting or CFO support. The introductory call is where we work this out with you.
