Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping services for Chicago businesses

Bookkeeping at Mogeny LLC, a Chicago, IL financial management firm, means every transaction in your business is recorded, categorized and reconciled each month. You get books that are current and accurate, ready for reports, lenders and your tax preparer. We work with Chicago businesses in person and with businesses across the US remotely.

Monthly bookkeeping

What is included in monthly bookkeeping?

Monthly bookkeeping keeps a complete, accurate record of the money moving through your business. The exact list is set out in your proposal.

  • Recording and categorizing every sale, purchase and payment
  • Reconciling every bank and credit card account each month. Reconciling means matching your books to your statements line by line, so nothing is missing or counted twice.
  • Tracking what customers owe you and what you owe vendors
  • Keeping receipts, invoices and bills organized and easy to find
  • Closing the books at month end so the numbers stay fixed
  • Year-end books ready for your tax preparer
Catch-up bookkeeping

Are your books months or years behind?

Catch-up bookkeeping brings missing records up to date. We start from your bank and card statements, rebuild each month, and reconcile it. Once the books are current, monthly bookkeeping keeps them that way.

Call (773) 964-2446Ask about catch-up work
  1. 01

    Gather the statements

    We list every account and collect the statements for the months that are missing.

  2. 02

    Rebuild and reconcile

    We record and categorize each transaction and match every month to the statements.

  3. 03

    Keep it current

    From then on, monthly bookkeeping keeps the books up to date.

Your part

What do we need from you?

Bookkeeping works best when information arrives on time. This is what we usually ask for.

  • Access to your bank and credit card accounts, read-only wherever the bank offers it
  • Access to your accounting software, or permission to set it up
  • Receipts and invoices, sent as photos or files as they come in
  • Answers to a short list of questions each month about transactions we cannot identify
Questions

What do people ask about bookkeeping?

If your question is not here, call or text (773) 964-2446.

How often will my books be updated?

Monthly is the usual schedule. A business with a lot of transactions may need weekly updates. We agree the schedule with you before we start.

What is the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?

Bookkeeping is the daily and monthly work of recording and reconciling transactions. Accounting uses those records to prepare statements, analyze results and plan. Mogeny does both.

Can you catch up several years of books?

Yes. We usually start with the most recent year and work back as far as your tax preparer or lender needs.

Which accounting software do you work with?

Tell us what you use now on the first call. If you do not use any software yet, we will recommend a setup that fits your business.

Will you work with my tax preparer?

Yes. We keep the books ready for year end and can answer your tax preparer’s questions about them directly.
