Family office and estate management

Family office and estate management in Chicago

Mogeny LLC, a Chicago, IL financial management firm, provides family office and estate management for families whose money sits across several accounts, properties or entities. We track it, keep the records in order, help pay what is due, and report it to you in one clear view, working alongside your attorney and advisers.

Family office

What does family office support cover?

A family office is the team that runs a family’s financial administration, the way a finance department runs a company’s. Mogeny can provide that support for your family.

  1. 01

    Reporting across every account

    Bank, investment, retirement and property accounts brought together in one regular report, so the family sees the whole picture.

  2. 02

    Household bookkeeping and bills

    Household spending recorded, recurring bills tracked and paid on time, and records kept ready for tax time.

  3. 03

    Properties and family entities

    Rental properties, family LLCs and trusts each tracked separately and correctly. An entity is a separate legal body, such as an LLC or a trust, that keeps its own records.

  4. 04

    Records ready for your advisers

    Organized records for your tax preparer, attorney and investment adviser, so each of them has the numbers they need.

Estate management

How can Mogeny help with an estate?

An executor or trustee is the person named to manage an estate or a trust. They have to account for everything that comes in and goes out. We keep those financial records for them.

Your advisers

Who else do we work with?

We handle the accounting and the administration. Legal advice stays with your attorney. Investment decisions stay with your investment adviser. Tax returns stay with your tax preparer.

Our job is to make sure each of them has accurate numbers when they need them.

Privacy

How is your family’s information kept private?

Family finances are private. We share information only with the people you name, and we never ask for passwords or account numbers through this website.

Questions

What do people ask about family office and estate management?

If your question is not here, call or text (773) 964-2446.

Is family office support only for very wealthy families?

No. It helps any family with several accounts, properties or entities to keep track of. What matters is how complicated the finances are.

Can you help an executor who has never done this before?

Yes. We keep the financial records for the estate and work with the estate’s attorney, so the executor knows what has been paid, what is left and what still needs to happen.

Do you give investment or legal advice?

No. We work alongside your investment adviser and your attorney and give them accurate numbers. Decisions about investments and legal matters stay with them and with you.

Can you manage the finances of an aging parent?

Yes, with the right legal authority in place, such as a power of attorney. We can track the accounts, pay the bills and report to the family.

Can we meet in person?

Yes, if you are in Chicago. Families elsewhere in the US work with us by video call, phone and email.
