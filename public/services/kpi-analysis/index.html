Data-driven KPI analysis

Data-driven KPI analysis for Chicago businesses

KPI analysis at Mogeny LLC, a Chicago, IL financial management firm, picks the few numbers that show how your business is doing and tracks them every month. A KPI, or key performance indicator, is a number tied to a goal, such as gross margin or days to get paid. We explain what moves each one.

Common KPIs

Which KPIs should your business track?

The right set depends on your goals. These are common starting points, and we choose the final list with you.

Margins

Profit

Shows whether each sale earns enough.

  • Gross margin, the share of each sale left after direct costs
  • Net margin, the share left after every cost
  • Profit by product, service or job
Liquidity

Cash

Shows whether the business can pay its bills.

  • Cash runway, how many months your cash lasts at the current rate
  • Days to get paid by customers
  • Days to pay vendors
Growth

Sales

Shows where revenue comes from.

  • Revenue by service line
  • Average sale per customer
  • Share of revenue from repeat customers
Efficiency

Costs

Shows where money goes as you grow.

  • Payroll as a share of revenue
  • Cost per unit or per job
  • Overhead each month
Data-driven

What makes the analysis data-driven?

Every KPI is calculated from your own accounting, sales and payroll records. When a number moves, we trace it back to the transactions behind it, so you know the cause before you act.

  1. 01

    Your goals first

    We start with what you want the business to do this year, then choose the measures that show progress toward it.

  2. 02

    Clean data

    KPIs are only as reliable as the books behind them, so we check the records first.

  3. 03

    Context every month

    Each KPI is shown against last month, last year and your target, with a note on what changed.

How it works

How does KPI analysis work?

  1. Agree the goals

    We talk through what you want from the business this year, such as more profit, steadier cash or growth.

  2. Pick the KPIs

    We choose the measures that show progress toward those goals, and define each one in plain words.

  3. Connect the data

    We pull the numbers from your accounting software, sales system and payroll.

  4. Report and explain

    You get the KPIs every month with a plain explanation of what moved and why.

Questions

What do people ask about KPI analysis?

If your question is not here, call or text (773) 964-2446.

What is a KPI?

A KPI, or key performance indicator, is a number that shows progress toward a business goal. Gross margin, cash runway and days to get paid are common examples.

Do I need special software for KPI analysis?

No. We start with the data you already have in your accounting software and spreadsheets.

How is KPI analysis different from financial reporting?

Financial statements show the whole picture. KPI analysis picks the few numbers that matter most for your goals and tracks them closely over time.

How often should I look at my KPIs?

Monthly suits most measures. Cash is often worth checking weekly. We agree the rhythm with you.
