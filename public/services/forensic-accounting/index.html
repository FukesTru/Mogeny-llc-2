Forensic accounting

Forensic accounting in Chicago

Forensic accounting at Mogeny LLC, a Chicago, IL financial management firm, means examining financial records closely to find out what happened to the money. We trace transactions, rebuild missing records, and set out what we find in writing for you and your attorney. Call when something in the numbers does not add up.

Situations

When do people need forensic accounting?

These are situations where a careful look at the records can help. Every matter is different, so we start by understanding yours.

Money seems to be missing

Sales are steady but cash is short, or payments go to vendors nobody recognizes.

A dispute between partners

Business partners disagree about money taken out of the business or spent without agreement.

A divorce

Business or household income and spending need to be shown clearly from the records.

An estate with gaps

Records are missing, or a family member questions how money in an estate was handled.

Books that will not reconcile

The accounts do not match the statements and nobody can explain why.

Concerns about an employee

Something about how an employee handles payments, refunds or expenses does not look right.

First steps

What should you do first if you think money is missing?

These steps protect the records and keep your options open.

  1. 01

    Leave the records as they are

    Do not delete, edit or tidy anything, in the books, the bank portal or email.

  2. 02

    Limit access where you can

    Restrict account access for anyone involved, where you have the right to do so.

  3. 03

    Write down what you noticed

    Note what you saw, when you saw it, and who else knows.

  4. 04

    Talk to your attorney

    If a dispute or legal action is possible, involve your attorney early.

  5. 05

    Call us

    Call (773) 964-2446 to talk through which records exist and what they might show.

The review

What does a forensic accounting review involve?

  1. Define the question

    We agree exactly what you need to know, for which accounts and which period.

  2. Gather the records

    Bank statements, accounting files, invoices, payroll and anything else that holds the answer.

  3. Trace and rebuild

    We follow each transaction from start to finish and rebuild records that are missing.

  4. Report the findings

    You get a written report that sets out what we found and how we found it.

Do you work with attorneys?

Yes. Forensic accounting often supports a legal matter. We can work directly with your attorney, and the attorney decides how the findings are used.

Is a forensic review confidential?

Yes. We treat every forensic matter as confidential and share findings only with you and the people you name.

Questions

What do people ask about forensic accounting?

If your question is not here, call or text (773) 964-2446.

What is forensic accounting?

Forensic accounting is the close examination of financial records to find out what happened, often when money is missing or disputed. The findings are written up so others can follow them.

How long does a forensic review take?

It depends on how many accounts and years are involved and how complete the records are. We give you an estimate once we understand the scope.

Do I need an attorney first?

Not always. If a dispute or legal case is possible, it is wise to involve your attorney early, and we are glad to work with them.

What records should I gather?

Bank and credit card statements, your accounting file, invoices, payroll records and any notes about what you noticed. We will tell you if anything else would help.
