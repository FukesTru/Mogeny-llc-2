Controller and CFO services in Chicago

The roles

Do you need a controller or a CFO?

A CFO, or chief financial officer, is the senior person responsible for a company’s finances. A controller runs the accounting underneath. Here is how the 2 roles divide the work.

Looks back

Controller

A controller runs the accounting and makes sure the numbers can be trusted.

  • Runs the month-end close
  • Reviews the bookkeeper’s work
  • Sets up internal controls, the checks that prevent errors and theft, such as a second approval on large payments
  • Reviews payroll and bills before they are paid
Looks ahead

CFO

A CFO uses those numbers to plan and to make the big financial decisions.

  • Forecasts cash for the months ahead
  • Builds the annual budget
  • Supports pricing, hiring and expansion decisions
  • Prepares for conversations with banks and investors

Many businesses need some of both. Mogeny can provide both roles, so planning starts from numbers that have already been checked.

Signs

When does a business need part-time CFO help?

These are the points where owners usually need more than bookkeeping.

  1. 01

    You spend more time on finance than on running the business.

    A part-time CFO takes the financial planning off your list, so your time goes back to customers and staff.

  2. 02

    Sales are up but cash is tight.

    A cash forecast shows when money will come in and go out, so a busy month does not turn into a cash shortage.

  3. 03

    You are raising money or taking on debt.

    Lenders and investors expect forecasts and clean statements. We prepare them and help you answer their questions.

  4. 04

    You are planning a hire, a new location or a large purchase.

    We work out what it costs and how it affects cash before you commit.

  5. 05

    Nobody reviews what the bookkeeper records.

    A controller review catches errors early, before they reach your reports or your tax return.

How it works

What does a part-time CFO arrangement look like?

Fractional, or part-time, CFO support means you get a CFO for a set number of hours or days each month instead of paying a full-time salary.

  1. Review where things stand

    We read your current books, reports and bank accounts and list what needs attention.

  2. Agree the priorities

    Together we decide what matters most right now, such as cash, pricing or a loan.

  3. Set the rhythm

    We agree the reports you get and how often we meet, in person in Chicago or by video call.

  4. Plan and adjust

    Each month we update the forecast, review results and help with the decisions in front of you.

How many hours a month will I need?

It depends on the size of the business and what is happening in it. We suggest a level after the introductory call, and it can change as your needs change.

Do I need a controller or a CFO?

If your main problem is numbers you cannot trust, start with a controller. If the numbers are right but you need help planning, a CFO fits. Many businesses need some of both.

Will you manage my current bookkeeper?

We can review your bookkeeper’s work and set the standards they follow. If you prefer, Mogeny can take over the bookkeeping too.

Can you join meetings with my bank or investors?

Yes. As part of CFO services we prepare the numbers for those meetings and can attend them with you.
